Events Sources Code

Bearly Missed.

Every few hours a scraper reads the places Berkeley events get posted and merges them into one list. Anyone can add a calendar or report an event. The code is on GitHub.

Sources

Tap a source to see how it's read.

Club Instagram

About 880 student-org accounts, taken from CalLink, the official club directory. Each club's recent posts are read, and the caption's date, time and room are pulled out by a rule-based parser ("GBM Thursday 10/29 7pm in Soda 306"). All clubs are read about every 9 days.

Club & department calendars

About 60 public calendar feeds: student-org Google Calendars found through CalLink and club websites, the Haas clubs calendar (around 40 business-school clubs), and departments and nearby institutions such as Berkeley Law, the iSchool, the Simons Institute, BAMPFA, the Botanical Garden and the Berkeley Public Library.

UC Berkeley Events

The campus calendar at events.berkeley.edu: talks, info sessions, performances and home games. Gym hours, daily exhibits and away games are left out.

Luma

Luma's discover feed around campus; about 25 campus-org calendars and Bay Area tech calendars; organizers who post from personal accounts; and any other Luma calendar found hosting near campus, which is added automatically.

Cerebral Valley

The Bay Area AI community calendar at cerebralvalley.ai.

Hackathons

Devpost, Y Combinator events, MLH and AGI House, kept to in-person Bay Area hackathons. Month-long online submission windows are skipped.

Music & venues

Concert calendars for Ashkenaz, the Ivy Room, the Fox, the Greek, the UC Theatre and more East Bay venues, plus Resident Advisor and DoTheBay.

Partiful

Bay Area trending events, plus any Partiful link that turns up in another source.

Reported by students

Links sent through the missing-event form. A Luma, Partiful, Meetup or Cerebral Valley link goes up on the next run; a Luma calendar link brings in every event on that calendar.

Mailing lists

Department and club listservs such as eecs-ugrads, read from an inbox subscribed to them. Event links in emails are fetched as full events, and announcements without a link go through the same parser as Instagram captions.

How sources are found

Most events come from finding the places clubs already post, then reading those places automatically. One discovery step often leads to many sources.

  • Bearly Missed
    • Club directories
      • CalLink, the official club directory
        • Instagram handle on each club's profile → ~880 accounts
        • Club website → public Google Calendar
      • Club sites on OCF, the student web host
        • Embedded Google Calendars → ~48 calendars in all
      • Haas CampusGroups → one feed, ~40 clubs
    • Event platforms
      • Luma discover feed around campus
        • Each calendar hosting near campus → added automatically
      • Cerebral Valley, Partiful
      • Devpost, YC, MLH, AGI House → hackathons
    • Campus & city calendars
      • events.berkeley.edu → every department
      • Law, Haas, iSchool, Simons, BAMPFA, library, museums
      • Music venues, Resident Advisor, DoTheBay
    • People
      • Missing-event form → links
      • Mailing lists → links and announcements

Any Luma, Partiful, Meetup or Cerebral Valley link found in a caption, email or report is followed to the full event, and a Luma calendar link brings in that whole calendar.

How it fits together
  • Duplicates are merged. The same event reached from several places, even under slightly different titles, becomes one card. The most complete listing wins, and the card shows every source it came from.
  • Only what's nearby. Events are tagged Campus, Berkeley, East Bay, San Francisco and so on; anything outside the Bay Area is dropped.
  • Polite scraping. Each source has its own schedule (from every 3 hours to once a day), requests to a site are spaced out, and unchanged calendars cost a single "not modified" check.
  • Nothing breaks silently. A source that fails keeps showing its last good events, and a broken source opens a GitHub issue automatically.

Code

Everything is at github.com/vitgruib/bearly: the scrapers in scraper/sources/, every calendar and club in sources.toml and clubs/instagram.txt, and this site in site/. The current data is public too: events.json.

Add a source

Anyone can add a calendar: a public Google Calendar, a Luma calendar, an .ics or RSS feed, or an events page. Once a maintainer approves it, it's checked every few hours like every other source. Two ways to send one:

  • On GitHub (free account). Fastest: a bot replies within a minute with the events it found, so a wrong link can be fixed right away.
  • With a Google Form (no account). The bot checks it within about half an hour, but can't reply to you; your club appears in the list below once it's approved.

See something wrong? Every event has a Report link for spam, cancellations and mistakes; an approved report takes the event down. For one event we missed, the missing-event form needs no account.
